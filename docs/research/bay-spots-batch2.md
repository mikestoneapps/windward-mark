Coordinates were placed by hand from the written descriptions and should be treated as ±100 m until checked on a map. Drive minutes are weekday mid-afternoon estimates (no routing call was made). ## Which candidates are real, regularly used kite launches, and under what rules?

### Takeaway
Three East Bay sites (Toll Plaza, Racetrack/Albany, Point Emery), two coast beaches (Dillon, Gazos Creek), one winter harbor option (Pillar Point), one Tahoe beach (Kings Beach) and two Folsom Lake launches are supported by local guideline pages or forum guidance; Marina State Beach is kept with low confidence as the only Monterey Bay launch with any kite evidence. The remaining candidates (Palo Alto/Shoreline, Redwood City, Treasure Island, Fort Bragg, Limantour/Drakes, Pescadero/Pomponio, Montara, Pacifica, Rodeo, Benicia, Brannan Island/Windy Point, Decker Island, Berryessa, Clear Lake, Lodi, Año Nuevo, Moss Landing, Del Monte) had no source showing regular kite use and were dropped.

### Cited Findings (one source per kept spot)
- **Toll Plaza / Radio Beach (Royce Beach)** — beginner+ school beach, April–September, mostly onshore SW (WSW/W/WNW/NW possible); dirt lot along the I-80 utility road floods at high tide; low tide = sand launch and up to ~100 yd of sticky mud, high tide = anchor/drift launch and "advanced"; live power lines at the south end, rocks and wind shadow at the radio-tower point, a kite once snagged on the tower; shallow bottom, poor for foiling; no toilets or water; VHF 16 for Coast Guard — [BAK site guide: Toll Plaza](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/toll-plaza/). A notch stronger than Alameda, crowded with KGB students — [BAK forum: Where to go post Alameda](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=75189).
- **Racetrack / Albany Beach (Albany Bulb, McLaughlin Eastshore SP)** — intermediate+ only, April–October (steadiest May–Oct, good late October), almost always onshore, usually SW, NW trickiest (Bulb wind holes); main launch is the Open Use Area by the Bay Trail with a short-leash anchor self-launch; Fleming Point beaches as an alternative with cleaner wind; hazards: barnacle rocks, old pier pylons off Fleming Point, driftwood, dogs/walkers at low tide, car break-ins; sensitive habitat in the North Basin cove and Albany mudflats; parking via Buchanan gate ($5, $1 Sundays) or Gilman St ($10 on race days, ask to waive) — [BAK site guide: Racetrack](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/racetrack/) and [BAK ?p=513](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=513). EBRPD stated in 2017 that it "does not intend to prohibit kitesurfing" after the Albany Beach restoration project but reserved the right to — [BAK forum: New Bay Trail will effectively prohibit kiting at Racetrack](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=96899). Golden Gate Fields parking advice ("Gilman, ~$4, SW/WSW good, launch behind the dune at high tide") — [BAK forum: where to kite this weekend (7/27)](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=25855).
- **Point Emery (Emeryville)** — small beach next to the frontage road usable for onshore launches — [BAK forum: where to kite this weekend (7/27)](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=25855); local guidelines (from batch 1's research): leash mandatory, not a beginner spot, launch at low tide, rocks/Bay Trail/I-80 downwind, Emeryville Marina channel, wind up 2–3:30 pm and dropping 4:30–5:30 — [BAK forum: East Bay Berkeley](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=51239).
- **Dillon Beach** — recommended as "the best place to start riding the coast": side/onshore wind, two break lines (larger first break a couple hundred yards out, smaller inside), not crowded; Gazos is the calmer alternative — [BAK forum: coastal kiting - when am I ready?](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93638). Bluff lots let you watch your gear — [BAK forum: Kiteboarding from a backyard](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=97082). Resort beach lot is paid day use (free with Golden Bear or Sonoma senior pass), dogs allowed, public fire pits, resort photo caption mentions kite surfers — [Dillon Beach Resort: Play](https://www.dillonbeachresort.com/play).
- **Gazos Creek State Beach** — "kiteboarding and windsurfing are popular here, and the beach is often windy"; two free lots just north and south of the gas station at Gazos Creek Rd/Hwy 1; strong rips, swimming not advised; snowy plovers — [That's My Park (State Parks partner): Gazos Creek State Beach](https://thatsmypark.org/?p=763). Calmer alternative to Dillon — [BAK forum](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93638).
- **Pillar Point Harbor (winter S winds)** — flat water during winter storm fronts; wind side-off, so stay upwind or you end up in the marina or on a moored sailboat; summer NW is ridden on the south end of the open beach instead — [BAK forum: [Coast] Half Moon Bay](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=94926).
- **Kings Beach SRA (Lake Tahoe)** — "the most consistent and predictable wind spot in the area", beginner-friendly learning spot, crowded on sunny days, 2013 reviews mention rocks at the south end at low water — [The Outbound: Learn to Kiteboard on the Lake](https://www.theoutbound.com/lake-tahoe/kiteboarding/100067). Fees $10/day or $3/hr year round, card only, annual pass accepted; lot 6 am–9 pm; windsurfing listed, kiteboarding not mentioned; no dogs on the beach; park coordinates 39.236716, −120.025012 — [CA State Parks: Kings Beach SRA](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=511). At altitude most riders fly 11–15 m in 15–25 mph; a 15–25 mph forecast is usually enough; SW is the storm-day direction — [BAK forum: Kiting on South Lake Tahoe](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=97465).
- **Folsom Lake — Granite Bay (south wind) and Folsom Point (north wind)** — Granite Bay "5% launch" on S/prefrontal days (right at the first stop sign, right at the T, kite upwind of the ramp; boulders just under the surface; add ~5 mph to the sensor); Folsom Point on N days (stay right in the park to the road's end, longer walk, self-land in the wind shadow; low water created a peninsula plus another ~100 yd downwind, so no big jumps or loops); typical 8–18 mph, good 19–28, 30+ rare; water 46–60 °F outside summer; rangers supportive; beginners OK on moderate days — [BAK forum: [Sacramento] Folsom Lake](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=27810). Day use $12/vehicle at both Granite Bay and Folsom Point, higher on peak days; windsurfing listed park-wide — [CA State Parks: Folsom Lake SRA](https://parks.ca.gov/?page_id=500).
- **Marina State Beach (Monterey Bay)** — "kited by others on northwesterly winds" — [BAK forum: Carmel / Pebble Beach](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=97699); launching/landing tricky because winds are mostly onshore, do not go out if caution tape is at the entrance, spring = strong clearing winds, summer/fall = light patchy thermals, only three or four local kiters, "Monterey is not a good place to learn" — [BAK forum: Anybody kite down at Monterey?](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=42739). Coordinates 36.68389, −121.81278; hang-gliding and surfing listed, kitesurfing not — [Wikipedia: Marina State Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_State_Beach).

### Inferences
- Toll Plaza, Point Emery and Racetrack are separate beaches on a 7 km stretch with the same SW–NW onshore window; the JSON gives them near-identical sectors and lets the notes differentiate tide and skill.
- Racetrack parking: Golden Gate Fields stopped racing in 2024, so the published gate fees and hours are almost certainly stale; the JSON says to check on the day.
- Kings Beach sectors (S–SW onshore) are inferred from the beach's north-shore position plus the forum's SW storm-wind note; no source gives a direction rose.
- Folsom is filed under region "Delta" because the schema has no Sacramento/foothills region; "Tahoe" or "South Bay" would be more wrong.
- Pillar Point's launch beach (west inner-harbor beach by the RV park) is placed by inference from "side-off, stay upwind" in a south wind; the thread names no lot.

## Direction window and season

### Takeaway
East Bay beaches run SW–NW onshore April–October; coast beaches are side-on NW spring–summer; Pillar Point harbor and Folsom Granite Bay are winter/prefrontal south-wind spots; Folsom Point is a north-wind spot; Kings Beach takes the afternoon southwesterly.

### Cited Findings
- Toll Plaza: April–September, mostly onshore SW, occasional WSW/W/WNW/NW — [BAK Toll Plaza](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/toll-plaza/).
- Racetrack: April–October, steadiest May–October, usually SW, NW tricky — [BAK Racetrack](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/racetrack/).
- Point Emery/Berkeley shore: almost always onshore, wind 2–3:30 pm, dropping 4:30–5:30, late season less predictable — [BAK Berkeley thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=51239).
- Dillon: side/onshore — [BAK coastal thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93638).
- Pillar Point harbor: winter south winds, side-off — [BAK HMB thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=94926).
- Folsom: N wind at Folsom Point (warm, sunny), S wind at Granite Bay (prefrontal, cloudy, gusty) — [BAK Folsom thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=27810).
- Marina/Monterey: strong clearing winds in spring, light patchy thermals summer–fall — [BAK Monterey thread](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=42739).
- Tahoe: SW 10–15 gusting 30 on a November storm day; 15–25 mph forecasts usually enough — [BAK Tahoe thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=97465).

### Inferences
- `off` sectors are set to anything with land behind the launch (E–SE on the East Bay beaches, NE–E on the coast beaches, N–NW inside Pillar Point harbor, N–E at Kings Beach, N at Granite Bay, S at Folsom Point).

### Gaps
- No month-by-month wind statistics were obtained for Kings Beach or Gazos (Windfinder page for "Kings Beach" resolved to South Africa).

## Hazards, parking and drive times

### Takeaway
All ten have free or ≤$12 parking; the East Bay sites' hazards are power lines, rocks, mud and tide-dependent beaches, the coast sites' are shore break, rips and sharks, and the lakes' are cold water and rocky shores far from the launch.

### Cited Findings
- Toll Plaza power lines (electrocution risk), radio-tower snag, mud, flooding lot — [BAK Toll Plaza](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/toll-plaza/).
- Racetrack barnacle rocks, pier pylons, break-ins, Buchanan $5/$1 Sun, Gilman $10 — [BAK Racetrack](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/racetrack/).
- Gazos rips, free lots — [That's My Park](https://thatsmypark.org/?p=763).
- Kings Beach $10/day, 6 am–9 pm, no dogs on beach — [CA State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=511).
- Folsom $12 day use, rocky shoreline, long swims — [CA State Parks](https://parks.ca.gov/?page_id=500); [BAK Folsom thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=27810).
- Pillar Point moorings/marina downwind — [BAK HMB thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=94926).

### Inferences
- Drive times in the JSON: Toll Plaza 35/25, Racetrack 30/30, Point Emery 35/25, Dillon 70/100, Gazos 115/80, Pillar Point 70/40, Kings Beach 215/230, Granite Bay 130/140, Folsom Point 135/140, Marina 145/115 (minutes from Paradise Cay / from Candlestick, weekday mid-afternoon, no traffic incidents). Tahoe and Monterey are at the edge of the 4-hour radius on a Friday afternoon.
