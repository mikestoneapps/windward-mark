# Bay Area kite launches within ~3 h of Paradise Cay (Tiburon) and Candlestick Point

Output: 16 spots in `/home/claude/research_notes/bay_spots.json` (schema identical to the app's `spots.json`, plus `tideStation`, `driveHome`, `driveSF`). Coordinates were placed by hand on the launch beach and the lot from the written descriptions below; treat them as ±100 m until checked on a map. Drive minutes are estimates for mid-afternoon on a weekday (no verified Google Maps call was made).

## Which of the candidate spots are legitimate, regularly used kite launches, and what are their rules?

### Takeaway
Fourteen of the candidates are established launches with published local guidelines (mostly on bayareakiteboarding.com); Candlestick and Rio Vista are rideable but contested, and Treasure Island, Paradise Beach Park, Larkspur/Corte Madera, Benicia, Point Pinole, Bolinas and Brannan Island have no evidence of being regular kite launches and were dropped.

### Cited Findings (one source per kept spot)
- **Crissy Field** — expert site; set up and launch west of the bathroom, launch toward the water with sand on the kite, no teaching/jumping/kites in the air on the promenade or lot; beachgoers always have right of way; launch upwind of windsurfers; VHF 16 / (415) 399-3451 for the Coast Guard — [BAK site guide: Crissy Field](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/crissy-field/). Zones (Inside, Anita Rock to channel, shipping channel, Presidio Shoal, Fort Point shadow, Last Chance Beach) — [BAK Crissy page](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=378).
- **Candlestick Point** — windsurf launch ("Windsurf Circle", Gate 1, SW corner just south of Hunters Point Expy, 135 ft carry through riprap, 19 free spaces, lot gate open 8–5 Sat–Wed and closed Thu–Fri); the Water Trail site report lists it as *not suitable for kiteboarding* — [SF Bay Water Trail Candlestick SRA report (2020)](https://www.sfbaywatertrail.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/CandlestickSRA_WaterTrailSiteDescriptionRpt_March2020.pdf). Launch is ~100 ft SW of the parking circle; W–WNW wind, flat water, offshore component can carry you toward Oakland — [iWindsurf forum: Candlestick](https://www.iwindsurf.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=147393).
- **Third Avenue / Baywinds Park** — two launches: upper mud-flat launch (beginners, lines perpendicular to wind, body-drag 300 ft) and lower beach below the lot (advanced); land only at designated sites (north cove between the two poles); 100 ft from others, 200 ft when jumping; keep lines off the bike path and out of the windsurf corridor — [BAK: 3rd Avenue/Baywinds Park](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=408); north-end kite area discussion — [BAK forum](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=91546).
- **Coyote Point** — "EXPERT ONLY KITE LAUNCH AREA"; designated inflation and launch zones, lines on the grass not the path, self-launch discouraged, slippery ramp at high tide, stay 200 ft off shore — [BAK site guide: Coyote Point](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/coyote-point/). County day-use fee applies at the park gate — [San Mateo County Parks: Coyote Point activities](https://parks.smcgov.org/coyote-point-recreation-area-activities) (fee amount not extracted; see Gaps).
- **Alameda (Crown Beach)** — launch/land only at the Shack (Boardsports California) and Shell Gate beach; citations for other spots; kite ambassadors in green/yellow hats; GPS 600 Westline Dr; park in the southernmost lot section; hazards: Toilet Bowl shadow at Ballena Bay, two flagged intake grates, Crab Cove, bird sanctuary, 300-yard mud walk at negative tides — [BAK: Alameda site](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=407); safety/right-of-way thread — [BAK forum](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=96896).
- **Berkeley** — listed as "Berkeley Marina Kite Launch" (separate from the windsurf launch) — [Kite & Windsurfing Guide: SF Bay Area](https://www.kite-and-windsurfing-guide.com/en/continents/countries/regions/san-francisco-bay-area). Local guidelines describe launching at the small beach by the Point Emery lot (Emeryville), leash mandatory, not a beginner spot, launch at low tide, rocks/Bay Trail/I-80 downwind, Emeryville Marina channel — [BAK forum: East Bay Berkeley](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=51239).
- **Point Isabel / Marina Bay** — small beach by the lot, 10–30 kn mostly onshore summer–fall, Brooks Island wind shadow right off the launch, rocky confined launch, "not a suitable location for beginners", bird nesting cove off-limits — [BAK forum: Point Isabel/Marina Bay](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8391).
- **Sherman Island** — guidelines by area (Upper Kite Beach, Middle Beaches, Playpen, Access/Pit = no kites, Baja, Baja Road launch with low power lines, County Park) and right-of-way rules — [BAK forum: Sherman Island Guidelines](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=1575). County park $8 vehicle day-use, year-round, sunrise–sunset — [Sacramento County Regional Parks: Sherman Island](https://regionalparks.saccounty.gov/us/en/parks/sacramento-river-and-delta/sherman-island.html). 16–30 kn, April–September, intermediate/advanced — [MACkite Sherman Island guide (2018)](https://www.mackiteboarding.com/news/-kiteboarding-travel-guide-sherman-island-california-usa-destinations-ep-15/).
- **Rio Vista / Windy Cove** — Rio Vista Windsurfing Association & Sherman Island Kiteboarding Organization (rvwa-siko) "maintains water access locations"; Delta Windsurf shop on Sherman Island — [Discover Rio Vista: Wind and Water](https://discoverriovista.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Rio-Vista-Wind-and-Water.pdf). No current page confirming Windy Cove as an open kite launch was found (see Gaps).
- **Bay Point (McAvoy Rd)** — launch 38.047908, −121.961793; park in the lot outside McAvoy Harbor; W/WNW ~20 mph, April–October, low–mid tide only, intermediate+, self-rescue mandatory — [BAK forum: Bay Point / end of McAvoy Road](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93830).
- **Stinson Beach** — listed as a kite spot — [Kite & Windsurfing Guide](https://www.kite-and-windsurfing-guide.com/en/continents/countries/regions/san-francisco-bay-area); local advice: sharky, cold, punchy beach break, fluky wind, starts ~11 am and "super funky by 3", an out-of-control kite in the public area could get the beach closed to kiting — [BAK forum: Bolinas lagoon thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=75432). NPS: gates open 9 am, seasonal closing, cars ticketed after close, lots fill before noon, prevailing NW best in the afternoon — [NPS Stinson Beach](https://www.nps.gov/goga/stbe.htm).
- **Bodega Harbor / Doran** — harbor kiting needs ≥2 ft tide; west-side park launch is offshore (downwinder to the east beach, chase car recommended); beach below Hwy 1 on the east side is "the best launch if you can go upwind", direct onshore, little room in front; sharks outside — [BAK: Bodega Bay](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=510).
- **Waddell Creek** — side-on waves favoring regular-footers, wind most summer afternoons, intermediate–advanced, cold water, heavy waves, cliffs — [MACkite Santa Cruz & Bay Area guide](https://www.mackiteboarding.com/news/kiteboarding-travel-guide-santa-cruz-and-bay-area-usa-destinations-ep-20-/); "stay out of the main break in front of the lot", ride around the creek mouth — [BAK forum: Where to find waves?](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=14345).
- **Half Moon Bay (Dunes Beach)** — Dunes and Venice best (wider, river-mouth waves), Miramar busy with cliff eddies, winter S winds ridden inside the harbor (side-off, stay upwind of moorings), summer NW along the whole bay — [BAK forum: Half Moon Bay](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=94926).
- **Ocean Beach** — advanced; no designated launch; north and south lots overlook the sand, most kiters use the north end; WNW onshore April–September, S–SE in winter storms; currents north on flood / south on ebb; Seal Rocks wind shadow; snowy plover area mid-beach — [BAK site guide: Ocean Beach](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/ocean-beach/).
- **Davenport** — listed as a spot in the regional guide — [Kite & Windsurfing Guide](https://www.kite-and-windsurfing-guide.com/en/continents/countries/regions/san-francisco-bay-area); no local guideline page found (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- Candlestick is included because the rider uses it, but its wind window is written conservatively (anything with north in it marked offshore) because the only launch description is a windsurf one and the Water Trail explicitly calls it unsuitable for kites.
- "Berkeley Marina / Hs Lordships" in the catalogue and the BAK Point Emery guidelines are two beaches ~2.5 km apart on the same shoreline; the JSON uses the Hs Lordships beach as the primary point and mentions Point Emery in the notes.
- Dropped spots: no kite-specific page or forum guideline exists for Treasure Island, Paradise Beach Park, Larkspur/Corte Madera, Benicia, Point Pinole, Bolinas Lagoon (the BAK "Bolinas lagoon" thread contains only Stinson advice) or Brannan Island.

### Gaps
- Coyote Point and Half Moon Bay State Beach fee amounts were not extracted (both are known fee parks; `pass: true`).
- Crissy Field East Beach parking: the BAK page says only "shared parking"; whether the Presidio now meters that lot was not verified.
- Windy Cove / Sandy Beach (Rio Vista): no current confirmation that the kite launch is open; flagged in notes to check rvwa-siko.org.
- Stinson: no source names a specific kite launch zone; the JSON places it at the north end of the NPS beach by inference.
- Davenport / Scott Creek: only a catalogue listing; notes are inferred from Waddell sources.

## Wind direction windows and season

### Takeaway
Bay and Delta spots run on W–NW (Bay) and W–SW (Delta) thermals from roughly April to September; ocean beaches are side-on NW in spring/summer and side-shore S–SE in winter storms.

### Cited Findings
- Bay Area best conditions "generally between April and September", summer is prime — [MACkite Santa Cruz & Bay Area guide](https://www.mackiteboarding.com/news/kiteboarding-travel-guide-santa-cruz-and-bay-area-usa-destinations-ep-20-/).
- Alameda: spring (Mar–May) 20+ mph W–NW; summer 12–15 mph W–SW; shifts up to 45° within minutes; season March–August — [BAK Alameda](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=407).
- Crissy: side-off at the launch, 3–5 pm historically most reliable, wind line can move offshore abruptly after mid-August; NW days make the inside workable; WSW + flood is a known hazard — [BAK Crissy](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/crissy-field/).
- Berkeley: almost always onshore; wind up 2–3:30 pm, dropping 4:30–5:30; late season less predictable — [BAK Berkeley thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=51239).
- Point Isabel: summer–fall, 10–30 kn, mostly onshore, Brooks Island shadow — [BAK Point Isabel thread](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8391).
- Sherman Island: 16–30 kn, almost daily April–September, June–August most consistent, Sept–Oct strong with fewer crowds; onshore at Upper Kite Beach, cross/onshore at the Middle Beaches — [MACkite Sherman](https://www.mackiteboarding.com/news/-kiteboarding-travel-guide-sherman-island-california-usa-destinations-ep-15/); [BAK Sherman guidelines](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=1575).
- Bay Point: W/NW/WNW ~20 mph, April–October — [BAK Bay Point](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93830).
- Ocean Beach: WNW straight onshore April–September; S–SSE side-shore in winter storms — [BAK Ocean Beach](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/ocean-beach/).
- Half Moon Bay: summer NW on the south end of the beach, winter S winds in the harbor — [BAK HMB thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=94926).
- Stinson: needs good NW flow, easy to get skunked — [BAK waves thread](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=14345).
- Candlestick: W to WNW typical — [iWindsurf Candlestick](https://www.iwindsurf.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=147393).

### Inferences
- The `dir` sectors in the JSON are derived from beach orientation plus these statements; `off` is set conservatively (any sector with land behind the launch, including Crissy's S, Candlestick's N–NE, Rio Vista's N–NE on the west bank).

### Gaps
- No source gives numeric compass windows for any spot; all sectors are inferred.

## Hazards

### Takeaway
Currents and shipping at Crissy, shallow water/mud and submerged grates at Alameda, slippery ramps and crowds at 3rd Ave/Coyote, tidal current and power lines in the Delta, and cold sharky surf with rips on the coast are the recurring hazards.

### Cited Findings
- Crissy: currents change first at the beach then spread; shipping channel (VHF 14), Presidio Shoal holes, Fort Point and North Tower wind shadows, piers downwind with ~2× current — [BAK Crissy](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=378).
- Alameda: beach drop-off at high tide, Toilet Bowl shadow, two concrete intake grates offshore, Crab Cove protected, 300-yard mud at negative tides, negligible current — [BAK Alameda](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=407).
- 3rd Ave: rocks, bike path, windsurf corridor, power lines, slippery mud; failure to engage the safety system is the most common injury cause — [BAK 3rd Ave](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=408).
- Coyote: slippery ramp at high tide, avoid high wind + high tide — [BAK Coyote](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/coyote-point/).
- Sherman: low power lines along Baja Road, rocks at Baja, holes in the sand, little room near shore — [BAK Sherman guidelines](https://bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=1575); submerged obstacles for foilers, logs on the beach — [MACkite Sherman](https://www.mackiteboarding.com/news/-kiteboarding-travel-guide-sherman-island-california-usa-destinations-ep-15/).
- Bay Point: thorny bushes, glass, snakes, fishing line, no downwind exit, possible helicopter evacuation — [BAK Bay Point](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93830).
- Ocean Beach: waves bigger than they look, rips alter apparent wind, Seal Rocks shadow — [BAK Ocean Beach](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/ocean-beach/).
- Stinson/Bodega: sharks, cold water — [BAK Stinson advice](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=75432); [BAK Bodega](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=510).
- Candlestick: gusts/lulls, offshore drift toward Oakland — [iWindsurf Candlestick](https://www.iwindsurf.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=147393).

### Inferences
- Delta tidal current at Sherman is well known locally but the fetched pages do not quantify it; the note keeps it qualitative.

### Gaps
- No source quantified currents at Sherman or Rio Vista.

## Parking, fees and tide stations

### Takeaway
Fees apply at Coyote Point (county), Alameda Crown Beach (EBRPD, seasonal), Sherman Island ($8), Sandy Beach Rio Vista (county), Half Moon Bay State Beach and Doran Regional Park; the rest are free lots or shoulders. Tide station ids were pulled from the NOAA CO-OPS metadata API.

### Cited Findings
- Sherman Island County Park: $8 vehicle, $15 with trailer, higher on summer holiday weekends, pay even when unattended — [Sacramento County Parks](https://regionalparks.saccounty.gov/us/en/parks/sacramento-river-and-delta/sherman-island.html).
- Candlestick Windsurf Circle: free, 19 spaces, lot closed Thu–Fri — [Water Trail report](https://www.sfbaywatertrail.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/CandlestickSRA_WaterTrailSiteDescriptionRpt_March2020.pdf).
- Alameda: park in the southernmost lot section, GPS 600 Westline Dr — [BAK Alameda](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/?p=407).
- Bay Point: park in the lot outside McAvoy Harbor — [BAK Bay Point](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=93830).
- Ocean Beach: north and south Great Highway lots — [BAK Ocean Beach](https://www.bayareakiteboarding.com/sites/ocean-beach/).
- Stinson: NPS lots, gates 9 am, ticketed after close — [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/goga/stbe.htm).
- NOAA CO-OPS tide-prediction stations (id, name; R = reference, S = subordinate), from `https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/mdapi/prod/webapi/stations.json?type=tidepredictions`: 9414290 San Francisco (Golden Gate) R; 9414358 Hunters Point R; 9414458 San Mateo Bridge west end R; 9414449 Coyote Point Marina S; 9414750 Alameda R; 9414816 Berkeley R; 9414843 Point Isabel S; 9415064 Antioch R; 9415316 Rio Vista R; 9415112 Mallard Island Ferry Wharf R; 9415020 Point Reyes R; 9414958 Bolinas Lagoon R; 9415625 Bodega Harbor entrance S; 9413745 Santa Cruz R; 9413878 Año Nuevo Island S; 9414131 Pillar Point Harbor R; 9414275 Ocean Beach outer coast S — [NOAA CO-OPS station metadata API](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/mdapi/prod/webapi/stations.json?type=tidepredictions).

### Inferences
- Reference (R) stations were preferred where one sits within ~10 km, so the app can request harmonic predictions; Point Isabel (9414843) and Bodega Harbor entrance (9415625) are subordinate stations chosen for proximity (swap to 9414816 Berkeley and 9415020 Point Reyes if the app needs reference stations only).
- Drive-time estimates (min) from Paradise Cay / from Candlestick: Crissy 25/25, Candlestick 45/5, 3rd Ave 60/25, Coyote 55/20, Alameda 50/35, Berkeley 35/30, Point Isabel 30/35, Sherman 85/80, Rio Vista 85/90, Bay Point 65/65, Stinson 45/70, Bodega 80/110, Waddell 110/80, HMB 65/40, Ocean Beach 35/30, Davenport 120/90. These are judgment estimates, not routed.

### Gaps
- Crown Beach, Coyote Point, Half Moon Bay SB, Sandy Beach and Doran fee amounts were not confirmed from an official page in this pass.
- No routed drive times (Google Maps was not queried).
